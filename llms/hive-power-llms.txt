# Hive Power

> Hive Power SA is a Swiss (Manno, Ticino) software company building FLEXO, a Flexibility Orchestrator that connects distributed energy assets — electric vehicles, stationary batteries, solar systems, and smart appliances — to electricity markets and grid services. FLEXO combines an AI optimization engine, a REST API mid-layer, and end-user/admin interfaces to deliver smart charging (V1G/V2G/V2X), virtual power plant (VPP) aggregation, battery monetization, energy-community management, and local optimization.

## About

- FLEXO is organized in three layers: an AI engine (weather/price/behavior forecasting, scheduling, optimization), an API mid-layer (programmatic access to energy usage, economics, notifications, and flexibility signals), and end-user interfaces (web admin portal + white-label apps).
- Hardware-agnostic integration via IoT platforms and manufacturer APIs (Enode, ABB, Huawei, LG, SMA, Pixii, Easee, ChargePoint, Free2Move eSolutions, Aurora's Grid, Solar Manager, SolarLog) and protocols MODBUS, MQTT, OCPP.
- The developer documentation portal is password-gated; no public OpenAPI, SDK, or package is published as of 2026-07-19.
